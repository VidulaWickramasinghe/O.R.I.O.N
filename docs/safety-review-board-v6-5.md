# O.R.I.O.N. v6.5 — Safety Review Board + Feature Approval Workflow

v6.5 adds local feature risk scoring, review decision tracking, required
controls, explicit development eligibility, reports, and approval packages.

Review records remain under `backend/data/safety_review_board/`. Approval is a
governance decision only: it never implements a feature, modifies GitHub,
pushes code, publishes releases, deletes files, exposes secrets, or bypasses
approval gates.
